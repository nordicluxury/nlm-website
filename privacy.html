Privacy Policy

Nordic Luxury Media — nordicluxurymedia.com
Effective date: 24 August 2026

1. Controller

The data controller for nordicluxurymedia.com is Nordic Luxury Media, a media company operating in Helsinki, Finland. Enquiries regarding this policy should be directed to contact@nordicluxurymedia.com.

2. Scope

This policy applies to all personal data processed in connection with your use of nordicluxurymedia.com and its subpages, including The Vantage editorial publication, the listing platform available at nordicluxurymedia.com/tilaa, and any downloadable reports published by Nordic Luxury Media. It does not apply to third party websites linked from this site.

3. Legal basis for processing

Nordic Luxury Media processes personal data on the following legal bases under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (the General Data Protection Regulation):

Consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR) — for the placement of analytics cookies and the processing of email addresses submitted voluntarily through site forms.

Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) — for the processing of order information necessary to fulfil services requested by the data subject.

Legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR) — for the retention of transaction records as required under applicable Finnish law.

4. Data collected and purposes

Analytics data. Subject to your consent, Nordic Luxury Media uses Google Analytics 4 to collect pseudonymous data regarding site usage. This includes pages visited, session duration, device and browser type, approximate geographic location at country level, and traffic source. This data is used solely for internal editorial and operational purposes. It does not identify you personally and is not used for advertising profiling.

Contact and enquiry data. When you submit a form on this site, including enquiries, listing orders or newsletter registrations, we collect the information you provide — typically your name, email address and the content of your submission. This data is processed for the purpose of responding to your enquiry or fulfilling your order and is not shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Order data. When you place an order through the listing platform, we collect your name, email address, company or agency name, listing URL and selected service package. This data is processed for the purpose of order fulfilment and retained for accounting purposes in accordance with Finnish bookkeeping legislation.

5. Cookies

This site uses cookies solely in connection with Google Analytics. No cookies are placed prior to obtaining your consent. If you decline consent, no analytics cookies are set and your visit is not tracked. You may withdraw consent at any time by clearing cookies in your browser settings.

6. Data processors and transfers

Nordic Luxury Media engages the following data processors:

Google Ireland Limited — analytics services. Data may be transferred to the United States under standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission. Privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Formspree Inc. — form submission processing. Privacy policy: formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

Stripe Inc. — payment processing for orders placed through the listing platform. Privacy policy: stripe.com/privacy.

Vercel Inc. — website hosting and content delivery. Privacy policy: vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy.

All processors are engaged under data processing agreements and are required to process data only on documented instructions from Nordic Luxury Media.

7. Retention

Analytics data is retained for a period of 14 months, after which it is automatically deleted in accordance with Google Analytics default settings. Email addresses and enquiry data are retained for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which they were collected, or until deletion is requested. Order records are retained for seven years in accordance with Finnish bookkeeping legislation (Kirjanpitolaki 1336/1997).

8. Data subject rights

If you are located within the European Economic Area you have the following rights under the GDPR: the right to access personal data held about you; the right to rectification of inaccurate data; the right to erasure; the right to restriction of processing; the right to data portability; and the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests.

Requests may be submitted to contact@nordicluxurymedia.com. Nordic Luxury Media will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman (Tietosuojavaltuutettu) at tietosuoja.fi.

9. Amendments

Nordic Luxury Media reserves the right to amend this policy at any time. The effective date at the top of this document will be updated to reflect any material changes. Continued use of the site following publication of an amended policy constitutes acceptance of its terms.
